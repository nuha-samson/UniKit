# UniKit 🎓

Basically, a one-stop dashboard for college students to keep track of their classes and deadlines without having to hunt through five different apps.

🔗 **Check it out:** https://uni-kit.vercel.app
🔗 **Code:** https://github.com/nuha-samson/UniKit

## What is this?

Let's be real: keeping track of every single assignment and course detail in uni is a headache. 

I built **UniKit** to put everything in one spot. I mainly wanted a project that let me actually practice the full-stack process—everything from the UI and database design to authentication and getting it live on the web.

## What it can do

* Add, edit, and organize your courses
* Keep a list of deadlines so you don't forget them
* Check things off as you finish them
* A personal dashboard just for your stuff
* Your data stays yours 
* Works on your phone/tablet
* It's actually live and working

## The Data

**Courses:** Stores the name, credits, semester, grade, and if you've finished it.

**Deadlines:** Stores the title, date, which course it's for, and if it's done.

Everything is tied to your account, so you won't see anyone else's grades or deadlines.
